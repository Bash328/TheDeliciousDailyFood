cuisine: "American"
description: "A lattice-topped pie of fresh cherries with a touch of almond, baked until the filling bubbles thick and glossy."
image: ""
recipeContent:
  prepTimeMinutes: 60
  cookTimeMinutes: 55
  servingsNumber: 8
  servingsUnit: Slices
  ingredients:
    - { amount: "1 recipe", item: "double pie crust (homemade or store-bought), chilled" }
    - { amount: "4 1/2 cups", item: "fresh cherries, pitted and halved or quartered" }
    - { amount: "2/3 cup (135 g)", item: "granulated sugar" }
    - { amount: "1/4 cup (28 g)", item: "cornstarch" }
    - { amount: "1 tbsp", item: "lemon juice" }
    - { amount: "1 tsp", item: "vanilla extract" }
    - { amount: "1/4 tsp", item: "almond extract" }
    - { amount: "1 tbsp", item: "cold unsalted butter, cubed" }
    - { amount: "1 large", item: "egg, beaten with 1 tbsp milk, for brushing" }
    - { amount: "Optional", item: "coarse sugar, for sprinkling" }
  steps:
    - "Stir the cherries, sugar, cornstarch, lemon juice, vanilla and almond extract together. Cover and chill while you roll the pastry."
    - "Roll one disc of chilled dough to a 30 cm circle and ease it into a 9-inch pie dish. Spoon in the cherries, leaving the juice in the bowl, and chill."
    - "Simmer the juice in a small pan over low heat for 3-4 minutes, until slightly thickened. Cool 5 minutes, pour over the cherries and toss gently. Dot with the butter."
    - "Roll out the second disc, cut it into strips and weave a lattice over the top. Press the ends to the bottom crust, trim and crimp the edge."
    - "Brush with egg wash, sprinkle with coarse sugar and chill 20-30 minutes while the oven heats to 200°C / 400°F."
    - "Bake on a baking sheet for 20 minutes, then lower to 190°C / 375°F and bake another 30-40 minutes, until golden and the juices have bubbled for at least 5 minutes. Shield the edges with foil if they brown fast."
    - "Cool at least 3-4 hours before slicing so the filling sets."
note: "Adapted from Sally's Baking Addiction's cherry pie (4.8 stars from 237 votes). Prep time doesn't include the 2 hours the pastry needs in the fridge."

image: ""
recipeContent:
  prepTimeMinutes: 75
  cookTimeMinutes: 30
  servingsNumber: 12
  servingsUnit: Pancakes
  ingredients:
    - { amount: "1/4 cup", item: "granulated sugar" }
    - { amount: "1 tbsp", item: "ground cinnamon" }
    - { amount: "4", item: "large eggs" }
    - { amount: "3 cups", item: "ice-cold water" }
    - { amount: "1/2 cup", item: "vegetable oil" }
    - { amount: "1/2 tsp", item: "salt" }
    - { amount: "2 cups", item: "all-purpose flour" }
    - { amount: "1", item: "lemon, cut into wedges" }
    - { amount: "2 tbsp", item: "vegetable oil, for the pan" }
  steps:
    - "Mix the sugar and cinnamon and set aside."
    - "Whisk the eggs, water and oil together. Sift the flour and salt into a large bowl, make a well and pour in the egg mixture while whisking. The batter will be very thin. Let it stand 30 minutes to 1 hour."
    - "Season your pan: heat 2 tbsp oil until it just begins to smoke, remove from the heat, pour off the oil and wipe the pan with a paper towel."
    - "Reheat over medium-high, add a thin layer of batter, and flip when bubbles form and the edges pull away. Cook until lightly browned."
    - "Sprinkle cinnamon sugar over each pancake and roll it up. Keep warm in a 175°F oven."
    - "Sprinkle with more cinnamon sugar and squeeze over lemon juice just before serving."
note: "Adapted from Feast and West's pannekoek (4.39 stars from 13 votes, a small sample)."

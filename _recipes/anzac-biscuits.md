title: "Anzac Biscuits"
date: 2026-11-25
category: Dessert
cuisine: "Australian"
description: "Golden oat and coconut cookies with a toffee-like flavor from butter and golden syrup. Crisp outside, a little chewy inside."
image: ""
recipeContent:
  prepTimeMinutes: 15
  cookTimeMinutes: 15
  servingsNumber: 16
  servingsUnit: Cookies
  ingredients:
    - { amount: "1 cup", item: "all-purpose flour" }
    - { amount: "1 cup", item: "rolled oats" }
    - { amount: "1 cup", item: "unsweetened desiccated coconut" }
    - { amount: "3/4 cup", item: "white sugar, ideally caster sugar" }
    - { amount: "150 g (5 oz)", item: "unsalted butter" }
    - { amount: "4 tbsp", item: "golden syrup" }
    - { amount: "1 tsp", item: "baking soda" }
  steps:
    - "Heat the oven to 180°C / 350°F (160°C fan) and line two baking trays with baking paper."
    - "Mix the flour, oats, coconut and sugar in a bowl."
    - "Melt the butter and golden syrup together in a saucepan, stirring. Stir in the baking soda; it will foam up. Take it off the heat right away."
    - "Pour the foaming butter into the dry mix and stir until just combined. It will look crumbly but holds when pressed."
    - "Press tablespoons of the mix into balls, flatten into patties and space them 2.5 cm apart on the trays. Thinner bakes crisper, thicker stays chewier."
    - "Bake about 15 minutes, swapping the trays halfway, until deep golden (12 minutes for chewy ones). Leave on the trays 5 minutes, then cool on a rack; they crisp as they cool."
note: "Adapted from RecipeTin Eats' Anzac biscuits (4.91 stars from 284 votes). No golden syrup? Light corn syrup or honey works, with a slightly different flavor."

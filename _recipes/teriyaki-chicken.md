  ingredients:
    - { amount: "4", item: "boneless, skinless chicken thigh fillets (about 5 oz each)" }
    - { amount: "1 tbsp", item: "oil" }
    - { amount: "2 tbsp", item: "soy sauce" }
    - { amount: "2 tbsp", item: "mirin" }
    - { amount: "2 tbsp", item: "sake (Japanese cooking wine)" }
    - { amount: "2 tbsp", item: "water" }
    - { amount: "To serve", item: "steamed rice" }
  steps:
    - "Combine the soy sauce, mirin, sake and water in a small saucepan over high heat. Boil, then simmer about 5 minutes until reduced by half to a light syrup. Watch it closely, since it thickens suddenly. Cool to room temperature."
    - "Coat the chicken with the sauce and marinate at least 20 minutes, ideally overnight. Lift the chicken out but keep the sauce."
    - "Heat the oil in a non-stick pan over medium to medium-high heat. If it's too hot, the sauce will burn before the chicken cooks through."
    - "Cook the chicken for about 3 minutes on the first side, then flip. If it browns too fast, take the pan off the heat and add a few tablespoons of water."
    - "Just before the chicken is cooked through, add 2 tbsp water and the leftover sauce to the pan and swirl so it forms a dark, glossy glaze. Turn the chicken to coat."
    - "Serve with rice."

---
title: "Shrimp stir fry "
slug: Shrimp-stir-fry
date: 2026-08-28T22:40:00.000+02:00
category: Lunch
description: Juicy shrimp and crisp, colourful vegetables tossed in a glossy
  sweet-and-savoury sauce, creating a quick, flavour-packed stir fry that’s
  perfect over fluffy rice.
image: /images/img_8130.jpeg
recipeContent:
  prepTimeMinutes: 15
  cookTimeMinutes: 10
  servingsNumber: 2
  servingsUnit: Servings
  ingredients:
    - amount: 300 g
      item: peeled and deveined shrimp
    - amount: 2 tablespoons
      item: vegetable oil
    - amount: 2 cloves
      item: garlic, minced
    - amount: 1 teaspoon
      item: grated fresh ginger
    - amount: "1"
      item: red bell pepper, sliced
    - amount: "1"
      item: carrot, thinly sliced
    - amount: 1 cup
      item: broccoli florets
    - amount: ""
      item: ½ cup snap peas
    - amount: 3 tablespoons
      item: soy sauce
    - amount: 1 tablespoon
      item: honey
    - amount: 1 tablespoon
      item: sesame oil
    - amount: 1 tablespoon
      item: lime juice
    - amount: ""
      item: ½ teaspoon chili flakes
    - amount: "2"
      item: spring onions, sliced
    - amount: 1 tablespoon
      item: sesame seeds
    - amount: ""
      item: Cooked rice, for serving
  steps:
    - In a small bowl, mix together the soy sauce, honey, sesame oil, lime
      juice, and chili flakes.
    - Heat 1 tablespoon of vegetable oil in a large wok or frying pan over high
      heat. Add the shrimp and cook for 2–3 minutes per side until pink and
      cooked through. Remove and set aside.
    - Add the remaining oil to the pan. Stir-fry the garlic and ginger for about
      30 seconds until fragrant.
    - Add the bell pepper, carrot, broccoli, and snap peas. Stir-fry for 3–4
      minutes until the vegetables are tender-crisp.
    - Return the shrimp to the pan and pour over the sauce. Toss everything
      together for 1–2 minutes until well coated and heated through.
    - Garnish with spring onions and sesame seeds.
    - Serve immediately over warm rice.
note: Cook the shrimp separately first and add them back at the end. This keeps
  them juicy instead of letting them become rubbery while the vegetables cook.
---

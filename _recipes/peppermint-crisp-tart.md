description: "A no-bake fridge tart of caramel cream, biscuits and crushed peppermint chocolate."
image: ""
recipeContent:
  prepTimeMinutes: 10
  cookTimeMinutes: 0
  servingsNumber: 10
  servingsUnit: Servings
  ingredients:
    - { amount: "2 cups (500 ml)", item: "heavy whipping cream" }
    - { amount: "14 oz (395 g)", item: "caramel (dulce de leche or caramel condensed milk)" }
    - { amount: "9 oz (250 g)", item: "Tennis biscuits or Arnott's Nice biscuits" }
    - { amount: "3", item: "Peppermint Crisp bars, crushed" }
  steps:
    - "Whip the cream in a large bowl until soft peaks form."
    - "Beat in the caramel until smooth."
    - "Arrange a layer of biscuits over the base of a square or rectangular dish."
    - "Spread over half the caramel cream and scatter with half the crushed Peppermint Crisp. Repeat with another layer of biscuits, cream and chocolate."
    - "Refrigerate overnight so the biscuits soften into a cake-like dessert."
note: "Adapted from Bake Play Smile's peppermint crisp tart (5 stars from 22 votes). Outside South Africa, use any plain coconut or sweet biscuit and peppermint chocolate."

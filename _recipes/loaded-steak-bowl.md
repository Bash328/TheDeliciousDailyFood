    - amount: 1 tsp

    - amount: 1 cup

    - amount: 1/4 cup

    - amount: 1 tbsp
      item: butter, melted
  steps:
    - Toss the diced steak and chicken with olive oil, garlic powder, smoked
      paprika, salt, and pepper.
    - Heat a skillet or grill pan over high heat. Cook the steak and chicken
      separately until browned and cooked through, about 5-7 minutes each. Set
      aside to rest.
    - Whisk the yogurt, minced garlic, and lemon juice together for the white
      sauce. In a separate small bowl, mix the hot sauce with the melted butter
      for the red drizzle.
    - Arrange the chopped lettuce, cucumber, tomato, and red onion in a large
      bowl.
    - Top with the cooked steak and chicken.
    - Drizzle with the white sauce and the hot sauce.
    - Serve immediately, tossing everything together at the table.

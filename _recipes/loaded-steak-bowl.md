  ingredients:
    - amount: 250g
      item: pasta (penne or fusilli)
    - amount: "4"
      item: boneless chicken thighs, diced
    - amount: 6 slices
      item: bacon, chopped
    - amount: 2 tbsp
      item: olive oil
    - amount: 1 tsp
      item: garlic powder
    - amount: 1 tsp
      item: smoked paprika
    - amount: ""
      item: Salt and pepper, to taste
    - amount: 2 cups
      item: romaine lettuce, chopped
    - amount: 1 cup
      item: cucumber, diced
    - amount: 1 cup
      item: tomato, diced
    - amount: 1/2 cup
      item: red onion, diced
    - amount: 1/2 cup
      item: plain yogurt or sour cream
    - amount: 1 clove
      item: garlic, minced
    - amount: 1 tbsp
      item: lemon juice
    - amount: 1/4 cup
      item: hot sauce
    - amount: 1 tbsp
      item: butter, melted
  steps:
    - Cook the pasta in salted water until al dente. Drain, rinse under cold
      water until cool, and set aside.
    - Toss the diced chicken with olive oil, garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt,
      and pepper.
    - Cook the bacon in a skillet over medium-high heat until crisp, then drain
      on paper towels. Cook the chicken in a skillet or grill pan over high heat
      for 5-7 minutes until browned and cooked through. Set aside to rest.
    - Whisk the yogurt, minced garlic, and lemon juice together for the white
      sauce. In a separate small bowl, mix the hot sauce with the melted butter
      for the red drizzle.
    - Spread the cooled pasta in the bottom of a large bowl. Arrange the chopped
      lettuce, cucumber, tomato, and red onion on top.
    - Top with the chicken and bacon.
    - Drizzle with the white sauce and the hot sauce.
    - Serve immediately, tossing everything together at the table.

title: "Creamy Carrot Soup"
date: 2026-11-25
category: Soup
cuisine: "American"
description: "Sweet carrots simmered in broth and blended silky with a little cream, topped with crisp bacon."
image: ""
recipeContent:
  prepTimeMinutes: 10
  cookTimeMinutes: 30
  servingsNumber: 4
  servingsUnit: Bowls
  ingredients:
    - { amount: "1 tbsp", item: "olive oil or butter" }
    - { amount: "4 slices (120 g)", item: "streaky bacon" }
    - { amount: "1", item: "onion, chopped" }
    - { amount: "2", item: "garlic cloves, minced" }
    - { amount: "5 large", item: "carrots, cut into 1.5 cm chunks" }
    - { amount: "4 cups (1 litre)", item: "vegetable or chicken broth" }
    - { amount: "1/2 cup (125 ml)", item: "cream, plus more for swirling" }
    - { amount: "3/4 cup (185 ml)", item: "milk" }
    - { amount: "To taste", item: "salt and pepper" }
    - { amount: "For garnish", item: "fresh thyme or parsley" }
    - { amount: "To serve", item: "crusty bread" }
  steps:
    - "Fry the bacon in the oil in a large pot over medium-high heat until golden. Lift out, cool and chop."
    - "Cook the onion and garlic in the bacon fat for 2 minutes, until soft but not browned. Add the carrots and stir to coat."
    - "Pour in the broth, cover and simmer briskly 20-25 minutes, until the carrots are very soft."
    - "Turn off the heat and blend until smooth with a stick blender, or in a blender once slightly cooled."
    - "Stir in the cream and milk and season. Thin with more milk or water if you like."
    - "Serve with a swirl of cream, the bacon and herbs, and crusty bread on the side."
note: "Adapted from RecipeTin Eats' creamy carrot soup (5 stars from 78 votes). Leave out the bacon and use vegetable broth for a vegetarian version."

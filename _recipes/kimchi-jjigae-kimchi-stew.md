title: "Kimchi Jjigae (Kimchi Stew)"
slug: kimchi-jjigae-kimchi-stew
date: 2026-01-01
category: Soup
cuisine: "Korean"
description: "A bubbling, spicy Korean stew of sour kimchi, pork belly and tofu."
image: ""
recipeContent:
  prepTimeMinutes: 10
  cookTimeMinutes: 35
  servingsNumber: 2
  servingsUnit: Bowls
  ingredients:
    - { amount: "2 cups", item: "packed bite-size kimchi, fully fermented" }
    - { amount: "4 oz", item: "fresh pork belly (or other pork with some fat)" }
    - { amount: "1 to 3 tsp", item: "gochugaru (Korean red chili flakes), to taste" }
    - { amount: "1 tsp", item: "minced garlic" }
    - { amount: "1 tbsp", item: "cooking oil" }
    - { amount: "1/2 cup", item: "kimchi juice (if available)" }
    - { amount: "2 to 2 1/2 cups", item: "water or broth" }
    - { amount: "6 oz", item: "tofu, sliced 1/2 inch thick" }
    - { amount: "2", item: "scallions, roughly chopped" }
    - { amount: "To taste", item: "salt (or soy sauce) and pepper" }
  steps:
    - "Cut the kimchi into bite-size pieces, the pork into bite-size pieces, the tofu into slices and roughly chop the scallions."
    - "Heat the oil in a small to medium pot and cook the kimchi, pork, chili flakes and garlic over medium-high heat 5-7 minutes until the kimchi softens and the pork is cooked."
    - "Add the kimchi juice and 2-2 1/2 cups water or broth and bring to a boil. Boil 5 minutes, then lower to medium and boil covered about 15 minutes, adding more water if needed."
    - "Add the tofu and scallions, season with salt or soy sauce and pepper (salt is usually not needed if the kimchi is well seasoned), and boil about 5 minutes until the tofu is cooked."
    - "Serve bubbling hot with rice."
    - "For a meatless version, skip the pork and add a can of tuna with its oil along with the water, boiling 10-15 minutes."
note: "Adapted from Korean Bapsang's kimchi jjigae (4.74 stars from 226 votes). Old, sour kimchi makes the best stew."

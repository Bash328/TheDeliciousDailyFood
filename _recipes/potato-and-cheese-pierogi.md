---
title: "Potato and Cheese Pierogi"
date: 2026-11-25
category: Dinner
cuisine: "Eastern European"
description: "Soft homemade dumplings filled with cheesy mashed potato, boiled and tossed with buttery crisp bacon."
image: ""
recipeContent:
  prepTimeMinutes: 90
  cookTimeMinutes: 30
  servingsNumber: 12
  servingsUnit: Servings
  ingredients:
    - { amount: "2 lb", item: "russet potatoes (about 5 medium), peeled" }
    - { amount: "1/2 tsp", item: "salt" }
    - { amount: "2 tbsp", item: "unsalted butter, melted" }
    - { amount: "2 oz", item: "cream cheese, softened" }
    - { amount: "3/4 cup", item: "shredded mozzarella" }
    - { amount: "1 cup", item: "warm water" }
    - { amount: "1/4 cup", item: "whole milk" }
    - { amount: "2 tbsp", item: "sour cream, plus more to serve" }
    - { amount: "1 large", item: "egg" }
    - { amount: "3 tbsp", item: "light olive oil or vegetable oil" }
    - { amount: "1 1/2 tsp", item: "fine sea salt, plus 1 tbsp for the cooking water" }
    - { amount: "4 cups", item: "all-purpose flour" }
    - { amount: "4 oz", item: "bacon, chopped" }
    - { amount: "2 tbsp", item: "unsalted butter" }
  steps:
    - "Filling: boil the potatoes until fork-tender, about 25 minutes. Drain, cool 5 minutes and mash smooth with the salt, melted butter and cream cheese, then the mozzarella. Cover and set aside."
    - "Dough: whisk the warm water, milk, sour cream, egg, oil and salt. Mix in 2 cups flour, then add the rest 1/2 cup at a time, then a spoonful at a time, just until the dough stops sticking. Knead 2-3 minutes until smooth, cover and rest 30 minutes."
    - "Roll half the dough on a floured surface to just under 3 mm thick, keeping the rest covered. Cut 7.5 cm rounds and put 1/2 tbsp filling on each."
    - "Fold each round over and pinch the edges firmly shut, then crimp again to seal well. Repeat with the rest of the dough and the scraps."
    - "Fry the bacon until crisp, then melt in the butter and take off the heat."
    - "Boil the pierogi in salted water in batches until they float, about 2 minutes. Lift out and toss with the bacon butter. Serve with sour cream."
note: "Adapted from Natasha's Kitchen's pierogi (4.95 stars from 288 votes). Makes about 60. Freeze them uncooked on a tray, then bag them, and boil straight from frozen."
---

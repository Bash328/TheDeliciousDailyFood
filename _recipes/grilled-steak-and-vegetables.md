---
title: Grilled steak and vegetables
slug: grilled-steak
date: 2026-08-31T17:25:00.000+02:00
category: Dinner
description: Juicy, perfectly grilled steak paired with smoky, charred
  vegetables for a simple, hearty meal packed with savoury flavour.
image: /images/grilled-steak-and-vegetables.jpg
recipeContent:
  prepTimeMinutes: 15
  cookTimeMinutes: 15
  servingsNumber: 2
  servingsUnit: Servings
  ingredients:
    - amount: "2"
      item: beef steaks
    - amount: 1 tablespoon
      item: olive oil
    - amount: 2 cloves
      item: garlic, minced
    - amount: 1 teaspoon
      item: dried rosemary
    - amount: 1 teaspoon
      item: dried thyme
    - amount: ""
      item: ½ teaspoon salt
    - amount: ""
      item: ½ teaspoon black pepper
    - amount: "1"
      item: red bell pepper, sliced
    - amount: "1"
      item: zucchini, sliced
    - amount: "1"
      item: red onion, cut into wedges
    - amount: 1 cup
      item: broccoli florets
    - amount: 1 tablespoon
      item: olive oil, for vegetables
    - amount: 1 tablespoon
      item: butter
    - amount: 1 tablespoon
      item: fresh parsley, chopped
  steps:
    - Pat the steaks dry and rub them with olive oil, garlic, rosemary, thyme,
      salt, and black pepper. Let them rest for 10 minutes.
    - Toss the bell pepper, zucchini, red onion, and broccoli with olive oil and
      a pinch of salt and pepper.
    - Heat a grill or grill pan over high heat. Grill the vegetables for 3–5
      minutes per side until lightly charred and tender.
    - Grill the steaks for about 3–5 minutes per side for medium-rare, or longer
      depending on your preferred doneness.
    - Remove the steaks from the grill and top each with butter. Let them rest
      for 5 minutes before slicing.
    - Serve the steak alongside the grilled vegetables and garnish with fresh
      parsley.
note: Let the steak rest before cutting into it. This helps keep the juices
  inside the meat and gives you a more tender, flavourful steak.

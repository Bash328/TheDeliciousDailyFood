    - { amount: "4", item: "garlic cloves, minced" }
    - { amount: "1 tsp", item: "paprika" }
    - { amount: "3/4 tsp", item: "salt" }

  steps:
    - "Pat the chicken dry. Whisk the oil, lemon juice, garlic, paprika, salt, cardamom, cumin, cinnamon and pepper flakes in a large bowl, add the chicken and toss. If you have time, marinate in the fridge up to 24 hours."
    - "Heat a large heavy skillet over medium-high heat. Add the chicken in a single layer and cook without moving for 8 minutes."
    - "Flip the pieces and cook, stirring now and then, 4-7 minutes more until browned and cooked through."
    - "Serve with toum, vegetables and warm pita."

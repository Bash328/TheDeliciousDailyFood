---
title: "Garlic Butter Shrimp"
date: 2026-11-25
category: Dinner
cuisine: ""
description: "Shrimp seared hot and fast, then tossed in a garlicky white wine, butter and lemon sauce. Have bread ready for the sauce."
image: ""
recipeContent:
  prepTimeMinutes: 10
  cookTimeMinutes: 6
  servingsNumber: 4
  servingsUnit: Servings
  ingredients:
    - { amount: "500 g (1 lb)", item: "large shrimp, peeled and deveined, tails on" }
    - { amount: "1/2 tsp", item: "kosher salt" }
    - { amount: "1/2 tsp", item: "black pepper" }
    - { amount: "3 tbsp", item: "extra-virgin olive oil, divided" }
    - { amount: "3 tsp (about 4 cloves)", item: "garlic, finely minced with a knife" }
    - { amount: "1/4 cup", item: "dry white wine" }
    - { amount: "3 tbsp (40 g)", item: "unsalted butter, cubed" }
    - { amount: "1 tbsp", item: "lemon juice" }
    - { amount: "1 tbsp", item: "parsley, finely chopped" }
    - { amount: "To serve", item: "lemon wedges and crusty bread" }
  steps:
    - "Toss the shrimp with the salt, pepper and 1 tbsp of the oil and leave for 20 minutes, no longer."
    - "Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large non-stick pan over high heat. Sear half the shrimp for 45 seconds a side, then lift out. Repeat with the last 1 tbsp oil and the rest of the shrimp."
    - "Return all the shrimp to the pan, add the garlic and stir for 30 seconds."
    - "Pour in the wine and stir, scraping the pan, until it has mostly cooked off, about 30 seconds."
    - "Scatter in the butter, add the lemon juice and swirl until the butter melts into a sauce. Toss in the parsley and serve with lemon wedges and bread."
note: "Adapted from RecipeTin Eats' garlic prawns (4.98 stars from 79 votes). Mince the garlic with a knife; a crusher makes it burn."

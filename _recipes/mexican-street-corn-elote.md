  steps:

    - "Stir the sour cream, mayonnaise, cilantro, garlic, garlic powder, cayenne, cumin and lime juice together."
    - "Partly unwrap the corn and let it cool a few minutes, keeping the foil under each cob for serving."
    - "Brush the sauce generously over the corn, sprinkle with cotija, then top with chili powder and cilantro. Serve with lime wedges."

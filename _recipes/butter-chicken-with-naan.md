    - amount: 1 tbsp

    - amount: ""

  steps:
    - In a bowl, combine the yogurt, lemon juice, 2 tsp garam masala, cumin,
      coriander, turmeric, chili powder, half the garlic, and half the ginger.
      Add the chicken, toss to coat, and marinate for at least 1 hour (or
      overnight in the fridge).
    - Heat the oil in a large skillet or grill pan over high heat and cook the
      marinated chicken until charred and cooked through, about 8-10 minutes.
      Set aside.
    - In a large pot, melt the butter over medium heat. Add the onion and cook
      until soft, about 5 minutes.
    - Stir in the remaining garlic and ginger, and cook for 1 minute until
      fragrant.
    - Add the crushed tomatoes, remaining garam masala, and salt. Simmer for 10
      minutes, stirring occasionally.
    - Stir in the cream and the cooked chicken, and simmer gently for another
      5-8 minutes until the sauce is rich and thickened.
    - Taste and adjust seasoning. Garnish with fresh cilantro.
    - Serve hot with warm naan bread for scooping up the sauce.

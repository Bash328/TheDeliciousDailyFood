    - { amount: "1 cup", item: "packed chopped fresh cilantro" }
    - { amount: "1 cup", item: "green lentils, rinsed" }
    - { amount: "1 cup", item: "red lentils, rinsed" }

    - { amount: "1/4 cup", item: "long-grain rice, rinsed (or broken vermicelli)" }
    - { amount: "To serve", item: "lemon wedges" }
  steps:
    - "Heat the oil in a large Dutch oven over medium heat and cook the onion, celery and carrot with a pinch of salt for 5 minutes until softened."
    - "Add the garlic and spices and cook 1-2 minutes, stirring."
    - "Add the tomatoes, tomato paste, cilantro, both lentils and the chickpeas with a little more salt and cook 5 minutes, stirring."
    - "Pour in the stock, bring to a boil for 5 minutes, then turn to low, cover and simmer 45 minutes until the legumes are very tender, adding more stock or water if needed. It should be thick but still pourable."
    - "Stir in the rice and cook 15 more minutes until done."
    - "Serve with lemon wedges."

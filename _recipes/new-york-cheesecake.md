date: 2026-01-01
category: Dessert
tag: "Classic"
description: "A rich, creamy cheesecake on a graham cracker crust."
image: ""
recipeContent:
  prepTimeMinutes: 30
  cookTimeMinutes: 75
  servingsNumber: 12
  servingsUnit: Slices
  ingredients:
    - { amount: "1 1/2 cups", item: "graham cracker crumbs" }
    - { amount: "1/3 cup", item: "granulated sugar, for the crust" }
    - { amount: "6 tbsp", item: "butter, melted" }
    - { amount: "32 oz", item: "cream cheese, softened" }
    - { amount: "1 cup", item: "granulated sugar" }
    - { amount: "1 cup", item: "sour cream" }
    - { amount: "4", item: "large eggs" }
    - { amount: "1 tbsp", item: "lemon juice" }
    - { amount: "2 tsp", item: "vanilla extract" }
    - { amount: "2 tbsp", item: "cornstarch" }
  steps:
    - "Heat the oven to 325°F. Mix the crumbs, 1/3 cup sugar and butter and press into the bottom of a 9-inch springform pan. Bake 10 minutes."
    - "Beat the cream cheese until smooth, then beat in the 1 cup sugar, sour cream, lemon juice, vanilla and cornstarch."
    - "Beat in the eggs one at a time on low speed, just until combined."
    - "Pour over the crust and bake 60-70 minutes until the edges are set and the center still wobbles slightly."
    - "Turn off the oven and leave the cheesecake in with the door cracked for 1 hour."
    - "Chill at least 6 hours before serving."
note: "Room-temperature cream cheese and eggs are the key to a smooth, lump-free batter. Setting a pan of hot water on the rack below while it bakes helps prevent cracks."

description: Fluffy, golden pancakes bursting with juicy blueberries and bright
  orange zest, finished with a drizzle of maple syrup for a sweet and refreshing
  breakfast.

recipeContent:
  prepTimeMinutes: 10
  cookTimeMinutes: 15
  servingsNumber: 2
  servingsUnit: Servings
  ingredients:
    - amount: 1 cup
      item: all-purpose flour
    - amount: 2 tablespoons
      item: sugar
    - amount: 1 teaspoon
      item: baking powder
    - amount: ""
      item: ½ teaspoon baking soda
    - amount: ""
      item: ¼ teaspoon salt
    - amount: ""
      item: ¾ cup milk
    - amount: "1"
      item: large egg
    - amount: 2 tablespoons
      item: melted butter
    - amount: 1 teaspoon
      item: vanilla extract
    - amount: 1 teaspoon
      item: finely grated orange zest
    - amount: 2 tablespoons
      item: fresh orange juice
    - amount: 1 cup
      item: blueberries
    - amount: ""
      item: Butter or oil, for cooking
    - amount: ""
      item: Maple syrup, for serving
    - amount: ""
      item: Extra orange zest and blueberries, for topping
  steps:
    - In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking
      soda, and salt.
    - In a separate bowl, whisk together the milk, egg, melted butter, vanilla,
      orange zest, and orange juice.
    - Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and gently mix until
      just combined. Do not overmix.
    - Gently fold in the blueberries.
    - Heat a lightly buttered or oiled pan over medium heat. Pour about ¼ cup of
      batter into the pan for each pancake.
    - Cook for 2–3 minutes, until bubbles form on the surface and the edges
      begin to set. Flip and cook for another 1–2 minutes until golden brown.
    - Serve warm with maple syrup, fresh blueberries, and a little extra orange
      zest.
note: >-
  If using frozen blueberries, add them straight to the batter without thawing
  them to prevent the pancakes from turning purple and watery.

  For a stronger orange flavour, add an extra ½ teaspoon of orange zest rather than more juice. The zest gives you a much more concentrated citrus flavour without making the batter too thin.

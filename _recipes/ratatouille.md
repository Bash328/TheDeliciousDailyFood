title: "Ratatouille"
date: 2026-11-25
category: Dinner
cuisine: "French"
description: "A Provençal stew of eggplant, zucchini, peppers and ripe tomatoes, each cooked separately so they keep their texture."
image: ""
recipeContent:
  prepTimeMinutes: 30
  cookTimeMinutes: 45
  servingsNumber: 8
  servingsUnit: Servings
  ingredients:
    - { amount: "1 large (about 1 1/4 lb)", item: "eggplant, cut into 1 cm cubes" }
    - { amount: "6 tbsp", item: "extra-virgin olive oil, plus more to serve" }
    - { amount: "2 medium (about 1 lb)", item: "zucchini, cut into 1 cm cubes" }
    - { amount: "1 medium", item: "yellow onion, finely chopped" }
    - { amount: "1", item: "red, orange or yellow bell pepper, diced" }
    - { amount: "5 large", item: "garlic cloves, chopped" }
    - { amount: "5 large (about 1 3/4 lb)", item: "ripe tomatoes, diced, with their juices" }
    - { amount: "1 tbsp", item: "tomato paste" }
    - { amount: "2 tsp", item: "fresh thyme, chopped, plus more to serve" }
    - { amount: "3/4 tsp", item: "sugar" }
    - { amount: "1/4 tsp", item: "red pepper flakes (optional)" }
    - { amount: "1 1/4 tsp", item: "salt, divided" }
    - { amount: "3 tbsp", item: "fresh basil, chopped" }
  steps:
    - "Heat 3 tbsp oil in a large non-stick pan over medium heat. Cook the eggplant with 1/4 tsp salt, stirring often, until soft and starting to brown, 10-12 minutes. Set aside."
    - "Add 1 tbsp oil and cook the zucchini 3-4 minutes, until tender-crisp. Season with 1/4 tsp salt and set aside."
    - "Add the last 2 tbsp oil and cook the onion and bell pepper about 5 minutes, then the garlic for 3 minutes more, without browning."
    - "Add the tomatoes with their juices, tomato paste, thyme, sugar, pepper flakes and 3/4 tsp salt. Cook 8-10 minutes, until the tomatoes break down into a sauce."
    - "Return the eggplant, bring to a gentle boil, then simmer uncovered on low about 10 minutes, until the eggplant is soft."
    - "Stir in the zucchini for 1-2 minutes just to warm through. Adjust the seasoning, scatter with basil and thyme and drizzle with olive oil. Serve warm or chilled."
note: "Adapted from Once Upon a Chef's ratatouille (4.92 stars from 254 votes). Even better the next day."

description: "Golden chicken breast steaks in a sticky honey, garlic and soy glaze. About 12 minutes, start to finish."
image: ""
recipeContent:
  prepTimeMinutes: 4
  cookTimeMinutes: 8
  servingsNumber: 4
  servingsUnit: Servings
  ingredients:
    - { amount: "500 g (1 lb)", item: "boneless, skinless chicken breasts (2)" }
    - { amount: "To taste", item: "salt and pepper" }
    - { amount: "1/4 cup", item: "all-purpose flour" }
    - { amount: "3 1/2 tbsp (50 g)", item: "unsalted butter (or 2 1/2 tbsp olive oil)" }
    - { amount: "2", item: "garlic cloves, minced" }
    - { amount: "1 1/2 tbsp", item: "apple cider vinegar or white vinegar" }
    - { amount: "1 tbsp", item: "soy sauce" }
    - { amount: "1/3 cup", item: "honey (or maple syrup)" }
    - { amount: "To serve", item: "rice and steamed greens" }
  steps:
    - "Slice the breasts in half horizontally to make 4 thin steaks. Season both sides and coat in flour, shaking off the excess."
    - "Melt most of the butter in a large skillet over high heat, keeping about 1 tsp back. Cook the chicken 2-3 minutes until golden, then turn and cook 1 minute."
    - "Lower the heat to medium-high. Push the chicken aside, add the garlic and the last bit of butter and stir briefly as it melts."
    - "Add the vinegar, soy sauce and honey, shake the pan to combine and simmer 1 minute until slightly thickened."
    - "Turn the chicken in the sauce (add a splash of water if it gets too thick) and take off the heat straight away. Serve drizzled with the rest of the sauce."
note: "Adapted from RecipeTin Eats' honey garlic chicken breast (4.97 stars from 838 votes). Take it off the heat promptly; honey burns fast."

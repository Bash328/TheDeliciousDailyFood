recipeContent:
  prepTimeMinutes: 15
  cookTimeMinutes: 20
  servingsNumber: 17
  servingsUnit: Churros
  ingredients:
    - { amount: "For frying", item: "vegetable, canola or corn oil, 2 inches deep" }
    - { amount: "1 cup", item: "water" }
    - { amount: "3 tbsp", item: "unsalted butter, cut into 3 pieces" }
    - { amount: "1 1/2 tbsp", item: "granulated sugar" }
    - { amount: "1/2 tsp", item: "salt" }
    - { amount: "1/2 tsp", item: "vanilla extract" }
    - { amount: "1 cup", item: "all-purpose flour" }
    - { amount: "1", item: "large egg, room temperature preferred" }
    - { amount: "1 cup", item: "granulated sugar, for coating" }
    - { amount: "2 tsp", item: "ground cinnamon (optional)" }
  steps:
    - "Pour 2 inches of oil into a large pan and heat to 360-365°F."
    - "Meanwhile, heat the water, butter, sugar and salt in a saucepan, stirring, until the butter melts and it boils."
    - "Add the vanilla and flour, reduce the heat to low and stir until a ball forms. Take off the heat and cool 4-5 minutes."
    - "Mix in the egg until the dough is slightly thicker than mashed potatoes."
    - "Spoon the dough into a sturdy piping bag with a large star tip."
    - "Pipe 3-4 inch lengths into the hot oil, snipping with a knife or shears and keeping the tip close to the oil to avoid splashing. Fry 2-3 at a time, about 90 seconds per side, until golden."
    - "Drain on paper towels for a minute, then roll in the cinnamon sugar. Serve warm."
note: "Adapted from Spend With Pennies' churros (5 stars from 81 votes). Use a thermometer, since oil at 360-365°F is what gives crisp churros."

description: "A soft, chewy loaf studded with whole blueberries in every slice — good toasted with butter or plain, still warm."

prepTimeMinutes: 20
cookTimeMinutes: 45
servingsNumber: 12
servingsUnit: Slices
ingredients:
  - { amount: "3 1/4 cups", item: "bread flour, plus more for dusting" }
  - { amount: "2 1/4 tsp", item: "instant yeast (1 packet)" }
  - { amount: "1 1/2 tsp", item: "salt" }
  - { amount: "2 tbsp", item: "sugar" }
  - { amount: "1 1/4 cups", item: "warm water" }
  - { amount: "2 tbsp", item: "olive oil" }
  - { amount: "1 1/2 cups", item: "fresh blueberries" }
steps:
  - "Whisk together the flour, yeast, salt, and sugar in a large bowl. Add the warm water and olive oil and mix until a shaggy dough forms."
  - "Turn out onto a floured surface and knead 8-10 minutes until smooth and elastic. Gently knead in the blueberries a handful at a time, being careful not to crush them too much."
  - "Place the dough in a lightly oiled bowl, cover, and let rise in a warm spot 1 1/2-2 hours, until doubled."
  - "Punch down the dough, shape into a loaf, and place in a greased 9x5-inch loaf pan. Cover and let rise 30-45 minutes more, until puffy."
  - "Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Bake 40-45 minutes, until deep golden and the loaf sounds hollow when tapped."
  - "Cool in the pan 10 minutes, then turn out onto a wire rack to cool completely before slicing."
note: "Toss the blueberries in a spoonful of flour before kneading them in — it keeps their juice from streaking the whole dough."

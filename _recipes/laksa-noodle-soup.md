  steps:
    - "Simmer the stock, water and drumsticks, then cook on medium-high for 25 minutes until the chicken falls off the bone and the liquid reduces by about a third. Discard the skin and bones, shred the meat and set the broth aside."
    - "Mix the chili sauce ingredients (sugar, soy sauce, garlic, 1 1/2 tsp laksa paste, Sriracha, chili paste, oil) and let it sit 20 minutes."
    - "Heat the oil over medium-low heat and cook the garlic and ginger 20 seconds, then the lemongrass and chilies 1 minute."
    - "Add the laksa paste, turn the heat to medium and cook 2 minutes, stirring, until fragrant."
    - "Add the chicken stock, coconut milk, fish sauce and 2 tsp of the chili sauce, cover and simmer 10 minutes."
    - "Season with lime juice and fish sauce, add the tofu puffs and let it stand off the heat 5 minutes."
    - "Cook the noodles per the package. Divide them between two bowls, top with chicken and bean sprouts, pour over the broth and finish with garnishes. Serve the extra chili sauce on the side."

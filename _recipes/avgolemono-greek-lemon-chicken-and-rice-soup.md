date: 2026-01-01
category: Soup
cuisine: "Greek"
description: "A silky, lemony Greek soup thickened with eggs, with tender chicken and rice."
image: ""
recipeContent:
  prepTimeMinutes: 10
  cookTimeMinutes: 25
  servingsNumber: 4
  servingsUnit: Bowls
  ingredients:
    - { amount: "1 lb", item: "chicken tenderloins or breasts, cut into 1-inch strips" }
    - { amount: "1 1/4 tsp", item: "salt" }
    - { amount: "8 cups", item: "chicken broth" }
    - { amount: "3/4 cup", item: "long-grain white rice" }
    - { amount: "1/4 tsp", item: "white pepper" }
    - { amount: "3", item: "sprigs fresh dill, plus 2 tsp chopped dill" }
    - { amount: "1", item: "bay leaf" }
    - { amount: "5 tbsp", item: "fresh lemon juice (2 lemons)" }
    - { amount: "2", item: "large eggs, plus 2 large yolks" }
    - { amount: "1/2 cup", item: "heavy cream, plus more if needed" }
    - { amount: "To serve", item: "freshly ground black pepper" }
  steps:
    - "Season the chicken all over with the salt and let it sit 15 minutes."
    - "In a large pot, bring the broth, rice, white pepper, dill sprigs and bay leaf to a boil, then reduce to low, cover and cook about 5 minutes until the rice is al dente. Take off the heat, add the chicken, cover and let sit about 15 minutes until cooked through."
    - "Discard the dill sprigs and bay leaf, lift out the chicken and chop it when cool enough."
    - "Transfer about 3/4 cup of the rice to a blender with the lemon juice, eggs and yolks and blend until smooth. With the lid vent open and covered with a towel, blend in a ladle of hot broth, then another."
    - "Slowly pour the egg mixture into the pot, stirring. Add the cream and heat gently on low. Don't let it boil. Simmer until the rice is done."
    - "Stir in the chopped dill and chicken, adjust the seasoning, adding more cream if it's too lemony or water if it's too thick, and serve immediately, since it thickens as it sits."
note: "Adapted from Once Upon a Chef's avgolemono (4.86 stars from 149 votes). Keep it below a boil once the eggs are in, or it will curdle."

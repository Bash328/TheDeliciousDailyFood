cuisine: "French"
description: "Silky vanilla custard under a crackling caramelized sugar crust."
image: ""
recipeContent:
  prepTimeMinutes: 20
  cookTimeMinutes: 30
  servingsNumber: 5
  servingsUnit: Ramekins
  ingredients:
    - { amount: "1", item: "vanilla bean (or 1/2 tbsp vanilla extract)" }
    - { amount: "1/4 tsp", item: "salt" }
    - { amount: "2 cups", item: "heavy cream" }
    - { amount: "5", item: "large egg yolks, room temperature" }
    - { amount: "3/4 cup", item: "granulated sugar, divided" }
    - { amount: "6 cups", item: "water" }
    - { amount: "To serve", item: "fresh berries (optional)" }
  steps:
    - "Heat the oven to 325°F."
    - "Slice the vanilla bean open and scrape the seeds into a large bowl. Put the pod in a pot with the salt and cream and heat gently about 10 minutes until it simmers, without boiling. Discard the pod. (If using extract, stir it in now.)"
    - "Whisk the yolks with 1/2 cup sugar gently, without whipping in bubbles. Bring the water to a boil."
    - "Slowly whisk about a quarter of the warm cream into the yolks, then whisk in the rest."
    - "Set 5 ramekins in a 9x13-inch pan and pour in the custard almost to the top. Pour boiling water into the pan until it comes halfway up the ramekins."
    - "Bake 30-35 minutes until set with a slight jiggle in the center. Cool at room temperature, then chill at least 6-8 hours."
    - "Just before serving, sprinkle the remaining 1/4 cup sugar over the custards and caramelize with a kitchen torch until golden. Garnish with berries."
    - "No torch: chill at least 24 hours, then broil on the top rack 1-3 minutes, watching closely, then re-chill 10-20 minutes."
note: "Adapted from Chelsea's Messy Apron's crème brûlée (4.94 stars from 63 votes). Whisk slowly so the custard has no bubbles."

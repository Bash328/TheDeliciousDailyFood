image: ""
recipeContent:
  prepTimeMinutes: 5
  cookTimeMinutes: 15
  servingsNumber: 4
  servingsUnit: Bowls
  ingredients:
    - { amount: "4 cups", item: "water" }
    - { amount: "1 piece", item: "kombu (dried kelp), about 4x4 inches" }
    - { amount: "1 cup", item: "katsuobushi (dried bonito flakes), packed" }
    - { amount: "7 oz", item: "soft or silken tofu, cut into 1/2-inch cubes" }
    - { amount: "4 tbsp", item: "miso" }
    - { amount: "1 tbsp", item: "dried wakame seaweed" }
    - { amount: "1", item: "green onion, thinly sliced" }
  steps:
    - "Soak the kombu in the water for 30 minutes if you have time. Don't rinse it."
    - "Heat slowly over medium-low for about 10 minutes and remove the kombu just before it boils."
    - "Add the bonito flakes, bring back to a boil, then simmer for only 30 seconds. Turn off the heat and let the flakes sink for about 10 minutes, then strain. This is your dashi."
    - "Return the dashi to the pot and heat to a slow boil (about 205°F), then turn off the heat."
    - "Dissolve the miso in a ladle or sieve with a little dashi, then stir it in. Gently add the tofu."
    - "Add the wakame and green onion just before serving. Serve right away, and never boil the soup once the miso is in."
note: "Adapted from Just One Cookbook's homemade miso soup (4.85 stars from 625 votes). For a shortcut, use dashi powder or packets, and for vegetarian soup skip the bonito flakes."

    - { amount: "1 tbsp", item: "curry powder" }
    - { amount: "1 tsp", item: "white sugar" }

    - { amount: "1 tsp", item: "kosher salt" }

    - { amount: "2 tbsp", item: "cider vinegar" }

    - { amount: "2 tbsp", item: "peanuts, finely chopped" }

    - { amount: "To serve", item: "lime wedges, cilantro and sliced red chili" }
  steps:
    - "If grilling, soak the skewers in water for 2 hours."
    - "Mix the chicken with the curry powder, 1 tsp sugar, 2 tsp curry paste, 1 tsp salt and 1/4 cup of the coconut milk. Marinate at least 20 minutes, or overnight."
    - "Thread 4-5 pieces onto each skewer."
    - "Heat 1 1/2 tbsp oil in a large nonstick pan over medium-high heat and cook the skewers in batches, about 3 minutes per side, until golden."
    - "For the sauce, put the remaining coconut milk and all the sauce ingredients (except the chopped peanuts) in a saucepan over medium-low heat. Stir, simmer 5 minutes and adjust with water until pourable but slightly thick. Keep warm."
    - "Pour some sauce in a bowl and sprinkle with peanuts. Pile the skewers on a platter and finish with peanuts, cilantro and chili."
    - "Serve with rice and the sauce for dipping."
note: "Adapted from RecipeTin Eats' Thai chicken satay (4.97 stars from 514 votes). Natural peanut butter is thinner, so you need less water."

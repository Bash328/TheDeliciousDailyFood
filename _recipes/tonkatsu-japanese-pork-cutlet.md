title: "Tonkatsu (Japanese Pork Cutlet)"
date: 2026-11-25
category: Dinner
cuisine: "Japanese"
description: "Pork loin in a light, shatteringly crisp panko crust, fried twice and served with shredded cabbage and tonkatsu sauce."
image: ""
recipeContent:
  prepTimeMinutes: 15
  cookTimeMinutes: 15
  servingsNumber: 2
  servingsUnit: Servings
  ingredients:
    - { amount: "2", item: "boneless pork loin chops, about 1 cm (1/2 inch) thick" }
    - { amount: "1/2 tsp", item: "kosher salt" }
    - { amount: "1/8 tsp", item: "black pepper" }
    - { amount: "2 tbsp", item: "all-purpose flour" }
    - { amount: "1 large", item: "egg" }
    - { amount: "1/2 tbsp", item: "neutral oil, for the egg" }
    - { amount: "1/2 cup", item: "panko breadcrumbs" }
    - { amount: "3 cups", item: "neutral oil, for frying (about 5 cm deep)" }
    - { amount: "4 tbsp", item: "tonkatsu sauce" }
    - { amount: "1 tbsp", item: "toasted sesame seeds, lightly ground (optional)" }
    - { amount: "1/4 head", item: "green cabbage, very finely shredded" }
    - { amount: "1", item: "Japanese or Persian cucumber, cut into thin strips" }
    - { amount: "To serve", item: "sesame dressing and steamed rice" }
  steps:
    - "Toss the cabbage and cucumber together and keep cold. Stir the ground sesame into the tonkatsu sauce, or serve the sauce plain."
    - "Mist the panko with water and leave it 15 minutes to soften; this makes a lighter, fluffier crust. Whisk the egg with the 1/2 tbsp oil, and put the flour in a separate dish."
    - "Trim the pork and make a few small cuts through the white band between meat and fat on both sides so it stays flat. Pound lightly, press back into shape and season with salt and pepper."
    - "Coat in flour, shaking off the excess, then egg, then press into the panko. Let the cutlets sit 5-10 minutes."
    - "Heat the oil to 170°C (340°F). Fry one cutlet at a time for 1 minute a side, then rest it on a rack for 4 minutes while you fry the next. Skim out loose crumbs between batches."
    - "Raise the oil to 180°C (355°F) and fry each cutlet again for 30 seconds a side. Drain 2 minutes, slice into 2 cm strips and serve with the cabbage, sauce and rice."
note: "Adapted from Just One Cookbook's tonkatsu (4.68 stars from 314 votes). The resting time between the two fries is what keeps the pork juicy."
---

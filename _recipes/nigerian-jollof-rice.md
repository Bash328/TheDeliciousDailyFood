    - { amount: "1 1/2 cups", item: "water" }
  steps:
    - "Blend the onion, tomatoes, garlic, ginger, bell peppers and habaneros with about 1/4 cup water until smooth."
    - "Heat the oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat and cook the chopped onion until soft, about 5 minutes."
    - "Add the thyme and tomato paste and cook 5 minutes, then add the blended mixture and cook 20 minutes."
    - "Stir in the curry powder, bouillon, salt, pepper and bay leaves and cook 10 minutes more."
    - "Wash the rice until the water runs clear (about four washes), then stir it into the tomato base to coat."
    - "Add the broth and 1 cup of the water (more if needed). Reduce the heat to low, cover tightly with foil under the lid and cook 20-30 minutes, stirring carefully every 10 minutes."
    - "Rest off the heat for 5 minutes, fluff and serve."

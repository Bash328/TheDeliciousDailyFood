  servingsUnit: Crêpes
  ingredients:
    - { amount: "1 1/2 cups", item: "milk" }
    - { amount: "1 cup", item: "all-purpose flour" }
    - { amount: "2 tsp", item: "granulated sugar" }
    - { amount: "1/4 tsp", item: "salt" }
    - { amount: "3 tbsp", item: "unsalted butter, melted" }
    - { amount: "1/2 tsp", item: "vanilla extract" }
    - { amount: "3", item: "large eggs" }
    - { amount: "To cook", item: "butter for the pan" }
    - { amount: "To serve", item: "powdered sugar and fillings of your choice" }
  steps:
    - "Blend the milk, flour, sugar, salt, butter, vanilla and eggs on low until combined, about 10 seconds. Scrape the sides, then blend a few more seconds."
    - "Cover and chill 1 hour, or overnight."
    - "Heat a 10-inch nonstick skillet over medium heat and stir the batter gently."
    - "Lightly butter the pan, pour in a scant 1/4 cup of batter and immediately tilt and swirl to coat the bottom."
    - "Cook until golden underneath, then flip with a spatula and cook until golden spots appear on the other side."
    - "Transfer to a rack and repeat, lowering the heat if needed."
    - "Dust with powdered sugar and fold or roll, or fill and then fold."
note: "Adapted from Cooking Classy's crêpes (5 stars from 35 votes). Resting the batter makes them tender."

---
title: Pumpkin soup
slug: Pumpkin-soup
date: 2026-08-23T14:42:00.000+02:00
category: Soup
description: A creamy, comforting pumpkin soup made with tender pumpkin,
  aromatic garlic and onion, warming spices, and a touch of cream. Perfect for a
  cozy lunch or dinner.
image: /TheDeliciousDailyFood/images/f51d0cdf-0454-4e1a-aee7-2c13aad754db.jpeg
recipeContent:
  prepTimeMinutes: 15
  cookTimeMinutes: 30
  servingsNumber: 4
  servingsUnit: Servings
  ingredients:
    - amount: 1 kg
      item: pumpkin, peeled and cut into cubes
    - amount: "1"
      item: medium onion, chopped
    - amount: 2 cloves
      item: garlic, minced
    - amount: "1"
      item: medium carrot, chopped
    - amount: 2 tbsp
      item: butter or olive oil
    - amount: 4 cups
      item: vegetable or chicken stock
    - amount: 1/2 cup
      item: cream
    - amount: 1/2 tsp
      item: ground nutmeg
    - amount: 1/2 tsp
      item: ground black pepper
    - amount: 1/2 tsp
      item: salt
    - amount: 1/2 tsp
      item: ground cinnamon (optional)
  steps:
    - Heat the butter or olive oil in a large pot over medium heat.
    - Add the onion and carrot and cook for 5 minutes until softened.
    - Add the garlic and cook for another 30 seconds.
    - Add the pumpkin, stock, nutmeg, black pepper, salt, and cinnamon.
    - Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer for 20 minutes, or until
      the pumpkin is very soft.
    - Use a blender or immersion blender to blend the soup until smooth.
    - Stir in the cream and heat gently for 2–3 minutes. Do not boil.
    - Taste and adjust the salt and pepper as needed.
    - Serve hot with fresh bread, croutons, or a drizzle of cream.

title: "Skillet Strawberry Shortcake"
slug: strawberry-shortcake
date: 2026-06-01
category: Dessert
tag: "Baking"
description: "One buttery skillet biscuit, macerated berries, and softly whipped cream."
image: ""
prepTimeMinutes: 20
cookTimeMinutes: 30
servingsNumber: 8
servingsUnit: Servings
ingredients:
  - { amount: "2 cups", item: "all-purpose flour" }
  - { amount: "1/3 cup", item: "sugar, plus extra for topping" }
  - { amount: "1 tbsp", item: "baking powder" }
  - { amount: "1/2 tsp", item: "salt" }
  - { amount: "6 tbsp", item: "cold unsalted butter, cubed" }
  - { amount: "3/4 cup", item: "heavy cream, plus extra for brushing" }
  - { amount: "1 lb", item: "strawberries, hulled and sliced" }
  - { amount: "2 tbsp", item: "sugar, for the berries" }
  - { amount: "1 cup", item: "heavy cream, whipped, for serving" }
steps:
  - "Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Whisk flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a bowl."
  - "Cut in the cold butter with your fingers or a pastry cutter until the mixture looks like coarse crumbs."
  - "Stir in the cream just until a shaggy dough forms. Press into a buttered 9-inch skillet, brush with cream, and sprinkle with sugar."
  - "Bake 25-30 minutes until deep golden and a toothpick comes out clean. Cool 10 minutes."
  - "Meanwhile, toss the sliced strawberries with sugar and let sit at room temperature until juicy, about 20 minutes."
  - "Slice the biscuit into wedges and serve warm topped with macerated strawberries and whipped cream."
note: "Macerate the berries while the biscuit bakes — perfect timing, no extra planning."

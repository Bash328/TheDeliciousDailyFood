  steps:
    - "Peel and shred the potatoes, then rinse in cold water until it runs clear. Squeeze out as much moisture as you can in a towel."
    - "Toss with the salt and pepper."
    - "Heat the butter in a large non-stick or cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat. Spread the potatoes into an even layer."
    - "Cook without moving 6-7 minutes until the bottom is deep golden, flip in sections and cook 5 minutes more."
    - "Serve hot."

description: "Tall, buttery scones with a tender, flaky crumb, best warm from the oven."
image: ""
recipeContent:
  prepTimeMinutes: 20
  cookTimeMinutes: 10
  servingsNumber: 8
  servingsUnit: Scones
  ingredients:
    - { amount: "2 cups", item: "cake flour, spooned into the cup and leveled" }
    - { amount: "2 1/2 tsp", item: "baking powder" }
    - { amount: "2 1/2 tbsp", item: "sugar" }
    - { amount: "Scant 3/4 tsp", item: "salt" }
    - { amount: "1/2 cup", item: "cold unsalted butter, cut into 1/2-inch pieces" }
    - { amount: "1", item: "large egg" }
    - { amount: "2/3 cup", item: "heavy cream" }
    - { amount: "1", item: "large egg, beaten, for brushing" }
    - { amount: "1 tbsp", item: "demerara sugar (optional)" }
  steps:
    - "Heat the oven to 425°F with a rack in the middle and line a baking sheet with parchment."
    - "Whisk the flour, salt, baking powder and sugar. Rub in the cold butter with your fingertips until the mixture looks like coarse crumbs with pea-size bits of butter."
    - "Whisk the cream and 1 egg, make a well in the dry ingredients and pour it in. Mix with a spatula until the dough comes together. It should be slightly sticky but still handleable."
    - "Turn onto a lightly floured surface and knead gently a few times. Press into a 7-inch circle about 3/4 inch thick and cut into 8 triangles."
    - "Place on the baking sheet, brush with the beaten egg and sprinkle with demerara sugar if using."
    - "Bake 11-13 minutes until lightly golden and firm to the touch. Serve warm."
note: "Adapted from Once Upon a Chef's scones (4.8 stars from 69 votes). Don't swap the cream for milk. Cold butter is the key to flaky scones."

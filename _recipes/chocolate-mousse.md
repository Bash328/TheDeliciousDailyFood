description: "Light, airy dark chocolate mousse made with whipped cream and egg whites. No gelatin. Make it the day before."
image: ""
recipeContent:
  prepTimeMinutes: 20
  cookTimeMinutes: 0
  servingsNumber: 4
  servingsUnit: Servings
  ingredients:
    - { amount: "3", item: "eggs, cold" }
    - { amount: "125 g (4.5 oz)", item: "70% dark chocolate, chopped" }
    - { amount: "10 g", item: "unsalted butter" }
    - { amount: "1/2 cup", item: "full-fat cream" }
    - { amount: "3 tbsp", item: "caster sugar" }
    - { amount: "To serve", item: "whipped cream and chocolate shavings" }
  steps:
    - "Separate the eggs while cold, whites into a large bowl and yolks into a small one. Whisk the yolks until even."
    - "Melt the chocolate and butter in the microwave in 20-second bursts, stirring between, until smooth. Let it cool to room temperature but still pourable, about 40°C (104°F)."
    - "Whip the cream to stiff peaks, taking care not to overdo it."
    - "With clean beaters, whip the egg whites with the sugar to firm peaks that just flop over at the tip."
    - "Fold the yolks into the cream, about 8 folds. Pour in the chocolate and fold another 8 times; streaks are fine."
    - "Fold in a quarter of the whites to loosen it, then fold in the rest until no white patches remain, about 12 folds. Don't overwork it."
    - "Spoon into 4 small glasses and chill at least 6 hours, ideally overnight. Top with cream and chocolate shavings."
note: "Adapted from RecipeTin Eats' chocolate mousse (4.98 stars from 232 votes). It uses raw eggs, so choose very fresh ones and skip it for anyone pregnant or vulnerable."

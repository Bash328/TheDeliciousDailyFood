slug: baklava
date: 2026-01-01
category: Dessert
tag: "Middle Eastern"
description: "Flaky phyllo layers filled with spiced walnuts, finished with a lemon-honey syrup."
image: ""
recipeContent:
  prepTimeMinutes: 60
  cookTimeMinutes: 75
  servingsNumber: 30
  servingsUnit: Pieces
  ingredients:
    - { amount: "16 oz", item: "phyllo dough, thawed" }
    - { amount: "1 1/4 cups", item: "unsalted butter, melted" }
    - { amount: "1 lb", item: "walnuts, finely chopped (about 4 1/4 cups)" }
    - { amount: "1 tsp", item: "ground cinnamon" }
    - { amount: "1 cup", item: "granulated sugar" }
    - { amount: "1/2 cup", item: "honey" }
    - { amount: "2 tbsp", item: "lemon juice" }
    - { amount: "3/4 cup", item: "water" }
  steps:
    - "Thaw the phyllo overnight in the fridge, then 1 hour on the counter. Trim it to fit a 13x9-inch pan and keep it covered with a damp towel."
    - "Butter the pan (and line it with parchment if you like)."
    - "Boil the sugar, honey, lemon juice and water, stirring until the sugar dissolves, then boil 4 more minutes without stirring. Cool."
    - "Pulse the walnuts 10 times until finely chopped and toss with the cinnamon."
    - "Heat the oven to 325°F. Layer 10 phyllo sheets in the pan, brushing each with butter."
    - "Spread over a third of the nuts, add 5 more buttered sheets, then another layer of nuts. Repeat, ending with 10 buttered sheets and a brush of butter on top."
    - "Cut into 1 1/2-inch strips, then diagonally into diamonds. Bake 1 hour 15 minutes until golden."
    - "Right away, spoon the cooled syrup evenly over the hot baklava (it will sizzle). Rest uncovered at room temperature 4-6 hours or overnight."
note: "Adapted from Natasha's Kitchen's baklava (4.94 stars from 822 votes). Cool syrup on hot pastry is what keeps it crisp."

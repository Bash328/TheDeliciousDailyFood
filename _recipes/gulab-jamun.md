image: ""
recipeContent:
  prepTimeMinutes: 10
  cookTimeMinutes: 20
  servingsNumber: 14
  servingsUnit: Jamuns
  ingredients:
    - { amount: "1 1/4 to 1 1/2 cups", item: "sugar" }
    - { amount: "1 1/2 cups", item: "water" }
    - { amount: "4", item: "green cardamom pods, crushed (or 1/4 tsp cardamom powder)" }
    - { amount: "1 tsp", item: "rose water" }
    - { amount: "1 cup", item: "milk powder" }
    - { amount: "5 tbsp", item: "all-purpose flour (maida)" }
    - { amount: "1 large pinch", item: "baking soda" }
    - { amount: "1 tsp", item: "ghee or oil" }
    - { amount: "1 tbsp", item: "curd (yogurt) or 3/4 tbsp lemon juice" }
    - { amount: "2 to 4 tbsp", item: "milk, as needed" }
    - { amount: "For frying", item: "ghee or oil" }
    - { amount: "1 tsp", item: "chopped pistachios, to garnish" }
  steps:
    - "Boil the sugar, water and crushed cardamom until the syrup is just slightly sticky. Turn off the stove before it reaches one-string consistency (if it gets there, stir in 2 tbsp water). Add the rose water and keep it hot."
    - "Mix the flour, milk powder and baking soda, then rub in 1 tsp ghee."
    - "Stir the yogurt (or lemon juice) with 2 tbsp milk, add about 1 1/2 tbsp to the flour and begin bringing it together, adding a little more milk and yogurt only as needed for a smooth, soft dough that isn't soggy."
    - "Grease your hands and roll 14-18 smooth balls with no cracks, keeping them covered."
    - "Heat ghee or oil over medium heat, until a bit of dough dropped in rises slowly without changing color. The oil shouldn't be too hot, or they brown without cooking through."
    - "Fry the balls on medium for 1-2 minutes, then on low, turning gently, until evenly golden."
    - "Add them to the hot (not boiling) syrup and let them soak for 3 hours. Garnish with pistachios and serve."
note: "Adapted from Indian Healthy Recipes' gulab jamun (4.98 stars from 740 votes). Smooth, crack-free balls and moderate oil heat are the keys."

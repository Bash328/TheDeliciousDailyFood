cuisine: "Mexican"
description: "Pork shoulder slow-cooked with orange, cumin and oregano until it falls apart, then crisped in a hot pan for tacos."
image: ""
recipeContent:
  prepTimeMinutes: 15
  cookTimeMinutes: 420
  servingsNumber: 10
  servingsUnit: Servings
  ingredients:
    - { amount: "2 kg (4 lb)", item: "boneless, skinless pork shoulder" }
    - { amount: "2 1/2 tsp", item: "salt" }
    - { amount: "1 tsp", item: "black pepper" }
    - { amount: "1 tbsp", item: "dried oregano" }
    - { amount: "2 tsp", item: "ground cumin" }
    - { amount: "1 tbsp", item: "olive oil, plus more for crisping" }
    - { amount: "1", item: "onion, chopped" }
    - { amount: "1", item: "jalapeño, seeded and chopped" }
    - { amount: "4", item: "garlic cloves, minced" }
    - { amount: "3/4 cup", item: "orange juice (from 2 oranges)" }
    - { amount: "To serve", item: "tortillas, salsa, cilantro and lime" }
  steps:
    - "Pat the pork dry and rub all over with the salt and pepper. Mix the oregano, cumin and olive oil and rub that over too."
    - "Put the pork fat-side up in a slow cooker, scatter over the onion, jalapeño and garlic, and pour over the orange juice."
    - "Cook on low for 10 hours or high for 7, until tender enough to shred."
    - "Lift out the pork, cool slightly and shred with two forks. Skim the fat off the juices, and boil them down to about 2 cups if there's much more. They're salty; they season the meat."
    - "Heat a little oil in a large non-stick pan over high heat. Spread a batch of pork in the pan, drizzle with some juices and leave it until the juices evaporate and the underside is golden and crusty. Turn and briefly sear the other side. Repeat in batches without crowding."
    - "Drizzle with a little more juice and serve hot in tortillas."
note: "Adapted from RecipeTin Eats' carnitas (4.96 stars from 1,138 votes). The cook time shown is the high setting. It can also be done in a covered pot in a 150°C oven for about 5 hours."

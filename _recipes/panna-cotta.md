description: "A silky vanilla cream dessert that wobbles on the plate, with raspberry coulis if you like."
image: ""
recipeContent:
  prepTimeMinutes: 10
  cookTimeMinutes: 5
  servingsNumber: 6
  servingsUnit: Servings
  ingredients:
    - { amount: "2 1/2 tsp", item: "unflavored gelatin powder" }
    - { amount: "1/2 cup", item: "full-fat milk, for blooming" }
    - { amount: "1 cup", item: "full-fat milk" }
    - { amount: "1 1/2 tsp", item: "vanilla bean paste (or vanilla extract)" }
    - { amount: "1/4 cup", item: "superfine sugar" }
    - { amount: "1 pinch", item: "kosher salt" }
    - { amount: "1 1/4 cups", item: "heavy cream (not dolloping cream)" }
    - { amount: "For the moulds", item: "neutral oil spray" }
    - { amount: "8 oz (250 g)", item: "raspberries, fresh or frozen, for the coulis (optional)" }
    - { amount: "3 tbsp", item: "white sugar, for the coulis" }
  steps:
    - "Pour 1/2 cup of the milk into a small saucepan, sprinkle over the gelatin, whisk, and leave 5 minutes until it swells."
    - "Gently warm over medium-low heat, stirring, until the gelatin melts and the milk looks smooth. Don't let it simmer."
    - "Add the remaining milk, sugar, vanilla and salt and warm just enough to dissolve the sugar. Remove from the heat and let the vanilla infuse 10 minutes."
    - "Stir in the cream and pour into a jug."
    - "Refrigerate uncovered 1 hour, stirring at 30 and 45 minutes, until it's like pouring cream and the vanilla seeds stay suspended."
    - "Lightly oil six moulds (about 2/3 cup each), wipe out the excess, and divide the mixture between them."
    - "Refrigerate at least 6 hours, ideally overnight (4 hours is enough if not unmoulding)."
    - "To serve, run a knife around the edge, invert onto a plate and give it a firm shake until it drops out."
    - "For the coulis, simmer the raspberries and sugar 5 minutes, mashing, then blitz and strain. Spoon over the top."
note: "Adapted from RecipeTin Eats' panna cotta (4.96 stars from 22 votes, a small sample)."

recipeContent:
  prepTimeMinutes: 10
  cookTimeMinutes: 30
  servingsNumber: 4
  servingsUnit: Servings
  ingredients:
    - { amount: "1 cup", item: "uncooked sticky (glutinous) rice" }
    - { amount: "1 can (13.5 oz)", item: "full-fat coconut milk" }
    - { amount: "1/4 cup", item: "sugar" }
    - { amount: "1/2 tsp", item: "salt" }
    - { amount: "2 tsp", item: "cornstarch (optional, if the coconut milk is thin)" }
    - { amount: "3", item: "ripe mangoes, peeled and sliced" }
    - { amount: "To garnish", item: "toasted sesame seeds" }
  steps:
    - "Rinse the sticky rice twice, then soak in cold water for at least 1 hour or overnight. Rinse and drain."
    - "Put the rice in a heatproof bowl on a steamer rack over 2 inches of boiling water (the water shouldn't touch the rice). Cover and steam on medium 20-25 minutes until cooked through."
    - "Heat the coconut milk, sugar and salt in a saucepan until hot and the sugar dissolves, without boiling. If it's thin, thicken it with half a slurry of the cornstarch and 4 tbsp water."
    - "Pour 1 cup of the sauce over the hot rice and stir until it's absorbed. Let it cool to room temperature."
    - "Serve scoops of rice with sliced mango, a spoonful of the remaining sauce and sesame seeds."
note: "Adapted from Omnivore's Cookbook's mango sticky rice (5 stars from 9 votes, a small sample). Ripe, sweet mangoes make the dish."

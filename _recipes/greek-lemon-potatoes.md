title: "Greek Lemon Potatoes"
date: 2026-11-25
category: Dinner
cuisine: "Greek"
description: "Potato wedges roasted in lemon, garlic, oregano and broth until they soak up the flavor, then roasted again until golden at the edges."
image: ""
recipeContent:
  prepTimeMinutes: 10
  cookTimeMinutes: 70
  servingsNumber: 5
  servingsUnit: Servings
  ingredients:
    - { amount: "1.2 kg (2.5 lb)", item: "potatoes such as Yukon Gold, peeled" }
    - { amount: "1 1/2 cups", item: "low-sodium chicken broth" }
    - { amount: "1/2 cup", item: "olive oil" }
    - { amount: "1/3 cup", item: "lemon juice" }
    - { amount: "5", item: "garlic cloves, finely grated" }
    - { amount: "1 tbsp", item: "dried oregano" }
    - { amount: "2 tsp", item: "salt" }
    - { amount: "For garnish", item: "lemon wedges and fresh oregano" }
  steps:
    - "Heat the oven to 200°C / 390°F (180°C fan). Cut large potatoes into thick wedges about 3 cm across, and medium ones into thirds."
    - "Toss the potatoes in a roasting pan with the broth, oil, lemon juice, garlic, oregano and salt."
    - "Roast 20 minutes, turn the potatoes, and roast 25-30 minutes more, until the liquid is mostly absorbed and mainly oil is left."
    - "For crisp edges, move the potatoes to a second tray, spoon some of the oil from the first pan over them, and roast 35-40 minutes, turning once or twice, until golden."
    - "Put the first pan with its garlicky juices back in the oven for the last 5-10 minutes to reduce. Drizzle over the potatoes and serve with lemon wedges."
note: "Adapted from RecipeTin Eats' Greek lemon potatoes (4.95 stars from 317 votes). A side dish; it goes with roast chicken, lamb or souvlaki."

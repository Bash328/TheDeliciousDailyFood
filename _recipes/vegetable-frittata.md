  steps:
    - "Heat the oven to 425°F for the stovetop method (or 350°F to bake it in a 9x13 pan)."
    - "Whisk the eggs, dairy and salt just until blended, then whisk in half or all of the cheese."
    - "Warm the oil in a 12-inch oven-safe skillet over medium heat. Cook dense vegetables like onion first, add softer ones like zucchini, then garlic or greens until tender or wilted. Season with salt."
    - "Whisk the eggs once more and pour over the vegetables. Stir briefly to distribute, and sprinkle on any reserved cheese."
    - "When the edge turns lighter, about 30 seconds to 1 minute, move the skillet to the oven. Bake 7-14 minutes until puffed and the center only just jiggles."
    - "Cool on a rack, garnish with herbs and slice. (For the baked version in a buttered 9x13 pan at 350°F, bake 20-25 minutes.)"

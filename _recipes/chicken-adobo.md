cuisine: "Filipino"
description: "Chicken braised in soy sauce, vinegar and plenty of garlic until tender and glossy, the national dish of the Philippines."
image: ""
recipeContent:
  prepTimeMinutes: 10
  cookTimeMinutes: 50
  servingsNumber: 4
  servingsUnit: Servings
  ingredients:
    - { amount: "2 lbs", item: "chicken, cut into serving pieces" }
    - { amount: "4 tbsp", item: "soy sauce" }
    - { amount: "10", item: "garlic cloves, crushed" }
    - { amount: "4 tbsp", item: "cooking oil, divided" }
    - { amount: "3", item: "dried bay leaves" }
    - { amount: "1 tsp", item: "whole peppercorns" }
    - { amount: "1 1/2 cups", item: "water" }
    - { amount: "4 1/2 tbsp", item: "white vinegar" }
    - { amount: "1 tsp", item: "sugar" }
    - { amount: "To taste", item: "salt" }
    - { amount: "To serve", item: "steamed rice" }
  steps:
    - "Toss the chicken with the soy sauce and crushed garlic and marinate at least 1 hour (longer is better)."
    - "Lift the chicken out of the marinade and keep the garlic and the liquid separately."
    - "Heat 3 tbsp oil in a pot and brown the chicken about 2 minutes per side. Set it aside."
    - "Add the remaining oil to the pot and cook the garlic until it starts to brown."
    - "Return the chicken with the leftover marinade, peppercorns, bay leaves and the water. Bring to a boil, cover and simmer 30 minutes until the chicken is tender."
    - "Pour in the vinegar and boil 1 minute without stirring, then stir and simmer uncovered 15 minutes."
    - "Stir in the sugar and salt to taste, turn off the heat and serve hot over rice."
note: "Adapted from Panlasang Pinoy's chicken adobo (4.91 stars from 101 votes). Boiling the vinegar for a minute before stirring takes off its sharp edge."

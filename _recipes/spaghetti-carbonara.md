description: "Silky egg-and-cheese sauce clinging to spaghetti with crisp pancetta, with no cream."
image: ""
recipeContent:
  prepTimeMinutes: 10
  cookTimeMinutes: 20
  servingsNumber: 6
  servingsUnit: Servings
  ingredients:
    - { amount: "1 lb", item: "spaghetti" }
    - { amount: "6 slices", item: "pancetta, guanciale or bacon, chopped" }
    - { amount: "2", item: "garlic cloves, minced" }
    - { amount: "3", item: "large eggs, room temperature" }
    - { amount: "1 cup", item: "shredded Parmesan or Pecorino Romano" }
    - { amount: "To taste", item: "freshly ground black pepper" }
    - { amount: "To serve", item: "parsley or extra cheese" }
  steps:
    - "Whisk the eggs, cheese and plenty of black pepper in a bowl and set aside."
    - "Cook the pancetta in a large skillet over medium heat until crisp. Lift it out with a slotted spoon, then cook the garlic in the fat for 1 minute."
    - "Meanwhile, cook the spaghetti in salted water until al dente. Reserve 1 cup of the pasta water, then drain without rinsing."
    - "Toss the hot pasta in the skillet with the garlic."
    - "Take the pan off the heat and pour in the egg mixture, tossing with tongs. Loosen with splashes of pasta water as needed."
    - "Add the pancetta, toss, and season with salt to taste."
    - "Serve straight away with parsley or more cheese."
note: "Adapted from Spend With Pennies' spaghetti carbonara (4.99 stars from 501 votes). Take the pan off the heat before adding the eggs, so they make a sauce instead of scrambling."

recipeContent:
  prepTimeMinutes: 30
  cookTimeMinutes: 20
  servingsNumber: 4
  servingsUnit: Servings
  ingredients:
    - amount: 250g
      item: "Ground pork or chicken "
    - amount: 1 cup
      item: "Cabbage "
    - amount: "2"
      item: Spring onions (finely chopped)
    - amount: "2"
      item: "Clovers of garlic "
    - amount: 1 tsp
      item: Grated ginger
    - amount: 1 tbsp
      item: "Soy sauce "
    - amount: 1 tsp
      item: "Sesame oil "
    - amount: 1/2 tsp
      item: Salt
    - amount: 1/2 tsp
      item: "Black pepper "
    - amount: 1 tsp
      item: Sugar
    - amount: "1 tsp "
      item: "Cornstarch "
  steps:
    - >-
      Prepare the cabbage

      Finely chop the cabbage. Sprinkle with a small pinch of salt and leave for 5 minutes. Squeeze out excess water with your hands or a clean towel.
    - >-
      Make the filling

      Add the pork, cabbage, spring onions, garlic, ginger, soy sauce, sesame oil, salt, pepper, sugar and cornstarch to a bowl.


      Mix thoroughly for 2–3 minutes until the mixture becomes slightly sticky.
    - >-
      Fill the wrappers

      Place one wrapper in your hand. Put about 1 tablespoon of filling in the centre.


      Dip your finger in water and run it around the edge of the wrapper.
    - >-
      Fold

      Fold the wrapper in half over the filling and press the edges together firmly.


      You can keep them as simple half-moons—no fancy pleating required.
    - >-
      Prepare the steamer

      Line your steamer basket with baking paper with a few small holes poked in it, or lightly oil the surface.


      Make sure the dumplings aren’t touching each other, because they’ll expand slightly and can stick together.
    - >-
      Steam

      Bring about 2–3 cm of water to a strong simmer/boil.


      Place the dumplings in the steamer, cover with the lid and steam for 10–12 minutes.


      For pork, make sure the centre reaches 71°C. If you don’t have a thermometer, cut one open—the filling should be completely cooked through with no raw pink meat.
    - |-
      Serve
      Let them cool for a minute or two, then serve hot.
note: If you want really juicy dumplings, don’t overpack them. About 1
  tablespoon of filling per wrapper is the sweet spot.

slug: easy-banana-bread
date: 2026-01-01
category: Dessert
cuisine: "American"
description: "Moist, sweet banana bread with a tender crumb and golden crust."
image: ""
recipeContent:
  prepTimeMinutes: 15
  cookTimeMinutes: 60
  servingsNumber: 10
  servingsUnit: Slices
  ingredients:
    - { amount: "3", item: "very ripe bananas, mashed" }
    - { amount: "1/3 cup", item: "butter, melted" }
    - { amount: "3/4 cup", item: "granulated sugar" }
    - { amount: "1", item: "large egg" }
    - { amount: "1 tsp", item: "vanilla extract" }
    - { amount: "1 tsp", item: "baking soda" }
    - { amount: "1/4 tsp", item: "salt" }
    - { amount: "1 1/2 cups", item: "all-purpose flour" }
    - { amount: "1/2 cup", item: "chopped walnuts or chocolate chips (optional)" }
  steps:
    - "Heat the oven to 350°F and grease a 9x5-inch loaf pan."
    - "Mix the bananas and melted butter. Stir in the sugar, egg and vanilla."
    - "Sprinkle the baking soda and salt over the batter and stir. Add the flour and mix just until combined, then fold in the walnuts or chocolate chips."
    - "Pour into the pan and bake 55-65 minutes until a toothpick comes out clean."
    - "Cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a rack."
note: "The riper the bananas, the sweeter and more flavorful the bread. Freeze overripe ones for later."

Lemon Blueberry Muffins

The trick to a tall bakery-style dome is a hot oven for the first few minutes. Everything else is just good muffin math.

25mCook
40mTotal
12Muffins

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 3/4 cup granulated sugar, plus extra for topping
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 1/3 cup whole milk
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • 1 1/2 cups blueberries, fresh or frozen

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C). Line a muffin tin with liners.

  2. Whisk flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a large bowl.

  3. In a separate bowl, whisk eggs, oil, sour cream, milk, lemon zest, and lemon juice until smooth.

  4. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry and fold just until combined. Gently fold in the blueberries.

  5. Divide batter evenly among the liners, filling almost to the top. Sprinkle with sugar.

  6. Bake at 425°F for 5 minutes, then reduce oven to 350°F (175°C) without opening the door, and bake 18-20 minutes more until a toothpick comes out clean.

Toss frozen blueberries in a spoonful of flour before folding in — keeps them from sinking.

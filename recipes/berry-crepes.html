Berry Crepes with Whipped Cream

Thin, lacy crepes with just enough structure to hold a spoonful of cream and a handful of berries without falling apart.

Save to Pinterest
20mPrep
15mCook
35mTotal
4Servings

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 1/4 cups whole milk
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter, melted, plus more for the pan
  • 1 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 cup heavy cream, chilled
  • 2 tbsp powdered sugar
  • 1 cup strawberries, sliced
  • 1 cup raspberries

Method

  1. Whisk flour, eggs, milk, melted butter, sugar, salt, and vanilla together until completely smooth. Let the batter rest 15 minutes.

  2. Heat a lightly buttered nonstick skillet over medium heat. Pour in a thin layer of batter, swirling to coat the pan, and cook 1-2 minutes per side until lightly golden. Stack cooked crepes between sheets of parchment.

  3. Whip the cream with the powdered sugar to soft peaks.

  4. Fill each crepe with a spoonful of whipped cream and a few berries, then fold or roll.

  5. Top with the remaining whipped cream and berries just before serving.

Crepes keep well wrapped in the fridge for a day — reheat briefly in a dry skillet before filling.

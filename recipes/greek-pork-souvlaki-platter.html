Irresistible Greek Souvlaki: A Flavorful Feast!

Charred pork skewers, crisp fries, warm pita, and a cool spoonful of tzatziki — a taverna platter you can build at home.

20mPrep
15mCook
35mTotal
4Servings

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lbs pork shoulder, cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 4 pita breads
  • 1 lb frozen fries, cooked per package
  • 1 large tomato, sliced
  • 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
  • 1 lemon, cut into wedges
  • 1 cup tzatziki, for serving

Method

  1. Combine the pork with olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, oregano, cumin, salt, and pepper in a bowl. Toss to coat, cover, and marinate in the fridge at least 1 hour, up to overnight.

  2. Thread the pork onto skewers, soaking wooden skewers in water first if using.

  3. Heat a grill pan or outdoor grill to high. Grill the skewers, turning every few minutes, 10-12 minutes total until charred at the edges and cooked through.

  4. Meanwhile, cook the fries according to package directions, and warm the pita on the grill or in a dry skillet for a minute per side.

  5. Toss the red onion with the parsley and a squeeze of lemon.

  6. Divide the fries and warm pita among plates, lean the skewers alongside, and add the tomato slices and dressed onion. Serve with tzatziki and lemon wedges.

For extra charred flavor, finish the skewers over an open flame or under a hot broiler for the last minute.

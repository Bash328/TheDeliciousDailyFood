Skillet Strawberry Shortcake

One giant biscuit instead of individual shortcakes — less fuss, more butter surface area, and it looks better on a table anyway.

🍓
Save to Pinterest
20mPrep
30mCook
50mTotal
8Servings

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/3 cup sugar, plus extra for topping
  • 1 tbsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 6 tbsp cold unsalted butter, cubed
  • 3/4 cup heavy cream, plus extra for brushing
  • 1 lb strawberries, hulled and sliced
  • 2 tbsp sugar, for the berries
  • 1 cup heavy cream, whipped, for serving

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Whisk flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a bowl.

  2. Cut in the cold butter with your fingers or a pastry cutter until the mixture looks like coarse crumbs.

  3. Stir in the cream just until a shaggy dough forms. Press into a buttered 9-inch skillet, brush with cream, and sprinkle with sugar.

  4. Bake 25-30 minutes until deep golden and a toothpick comes out clean. Cool 10 minutes.

  5. Meanwhile, toss the sliced strawberries with sugar and let sit at room temperature until juicy, about 20 minutes.

  6. Slice the biscuit into wedges and serve warm topped with macerated strawberries and whipped cream.

Macerate the berries while the biscuit bakes — perfect timing, no extra planning.

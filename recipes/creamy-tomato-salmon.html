One-Pan Creamy Tomato Salmon

A short list of pantry staples turns into a sauce good enough to eat with a spoon — the salmon almost gets in the way.

Save to Pinterest
10mPrep
20mCook
30mTotal
2Servings

Ingredients

  • 2 salmon fillets, skin removed
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 3 tbsp tomato paste
  • 3/4 cup heavy cream
  • 1/4 cup chicken broth
  • 1/2 lemon, sliced, plus more for serving
  • 2 tbsp fresh oregano, chopped

Method

  1. Season the salmon with salt and smoked paprika. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat and sear the salmon 3-4 minutes per side, until just cooked through. Remove and set aside.

  2. Lower the heat, add garlic to the same pan, and cook 30 seconds until fragrant.

  3. Stir in the tomato paste and cook a minute to deepen its color, then whisk in the cream and chicken broth. Simmer 3-4 minutes until slightly thickened.

  4. Return the salmon to the pan, nestling in the lemon slices, and spoon the sauce over the top. Simmer 2 minutes more to warm through.

  5. Scatter with fresh oregano and serve straight from the pan.

The sauce is rich enough to serve over pasta or rice if you want to stretch it into a full plate.

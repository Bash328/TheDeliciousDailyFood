Ultimate Gourmet Grilled Cheese with Arugula

Deeply golden mushrooms folded into a creamy filling, roasted peppers and arugula, pressed between crisp, buttery sourdough.

Save to Pinterest
10mPrep
15mCook
25mTotal
2Servings

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp butter, divided
  • 8 oz cremini mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 small shallot, finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/4 cup cream cheese, softened
  • 2 tbsp sour cream
  • 1 roasted red pepper, sliced into strips
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 4 thick slices sourdough bread
  • 1 cup baby arugula
  • 1 tbsp olive oil, for toasting

Method

  1. Melt 1 tbsp butter in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add the mushrooms and cook, stirring occasionally, 6-8 minutes until deeply golden. Add the shallot and garlic and cook 1-2 minutes more until softened.

  2. Remove from heat and stir in the cream cheese and sour cream until the mushrooms are coated in a thick, creamy filling. Season with salt and pepper.

  3. Divide the mushroom mixture and roasted red pepper strips between two slices of bread, and top with a handful of arugula. Close the sandwiches with the remaining bread.

  4. Wipe out the skillet, melt the remaining butter with the olive oil, and toast the sandwiches 3-4 minutes per side, pressing gently, until deeply golden and crisp.

  5. Slice in half and serve warm.

The mushroom filling keeps well in the fridge for two days — reheat gently before assembling.

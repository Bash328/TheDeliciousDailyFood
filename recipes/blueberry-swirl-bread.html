Deliciously Easy Homemade Blueberry Bread Recipe

A soft, chewy loaf studded with whole blueberries in every slice — good toasted with butter or plain, still warm.

Save to Pinterest
20mPrep
45mBake
3hTotal
12Slices

Ingredients

  • 3 1/4 cups bread flour, plus more for dusting
  • 2 1/4 tsp instant yeast (1 packet)
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 1/4 cups warm water
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 1/2 cups fresh blueberries

Method

  1. Whisk together the flour, yeast, salt, and sugar in a large bowl. Add the warm water and olive oil and mix until a shaggy dough forms.

  2. Turn out onto a floured surface and knead 8-10 minutes until smooth and elastic. Gently knead in the blueberries a handful at a time, being careful not to crush them too much.

  3. Place the dough in a lightly oiled bowl, cover, and let rise in a warm spot 1 1/2-2 hours, until doubled.

  4. Punch down the dough, shape into a loaf, and place in a greased 9x5-inch loaf pan. Cover and let rise 30-45 minutes more, until puffy.

  5. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Bake 40-45 minutes, until deep golden and the loaf sounds hollow when tapped.

  6. Cool in the pan 10 minutes, then turn out onto a wire rack to cool completely before slicing.

Toss the blueberries in a spoonful of flour before kneading them in — it keeps their juice from streaking the whole dough.

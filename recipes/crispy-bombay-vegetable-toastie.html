Crispy Bombay Vegetable Toastie

A spiced vegetable filling pressed between buttered, cheese-melted bread until deeply crisp — finished with a cool drizzle and a scatter of chili flakes.

Save to Pinterest
15mPrep
10mCook
25mTotal
4Servings

Ingredients

  • 2 medium potatoes, boiled and mashed
  • 1/2 cup shredded cabbage
  • 1/4 cup grated carrot
  • 1/4 cup frozen peas, thawed
  • 1/2 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 green chile, finely chopped (optional)
  • 2 tbsp fresh cilantro, chopped, plus more to serve
  • 1 tsp chaat masala
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • Salt, to taste
  • 8 slices white sandwich bread
  • 4 tbsp green chutney (mint-cilantro), or store-bought
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 3 tbsp butter, softened
  • 1/4 cup sour cream or yogurt, thinned with a splash of water
  • Chili flakes, for serving

Method

  1. In a bowl, mix the mashed potato with the cabbage, carrot, peas, onion, chile, cilantro, chaat masala, cumin, and a pinch of salt.

  2. Spread one side of each bread slice with green chutney. Divide the vegetable filling and cheese between four slices, and top with the remaining bread, chutney-side down.

  3. Butter the outsides of the sandwiches. Toast in a hot skillet or panini press 3-4 minutes per side, pressing down, until deeply golden and crisp and the cheese has melted.

  4. Slice each sandwich into triangles and stack on a plate.

  5. Drizzle with the thinned sour cream, scatter with cilantro and chili flakes, and serve hot.

A panini press or sandwich toaster gets the crispiest, most even crust — a skillet with a heavy pan pressed on top works nearly as well.

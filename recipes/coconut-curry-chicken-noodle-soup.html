Coconut Curry Chicken Noodle Soup

A coconut curry broth rich enough to eat with a spoon, built around whatever noodles and vegetables are already in the fridge.

Save to Pinterest
15mPrep
25mCook
40mTotal
4Servings

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 lb boneless chicken thighs, cut into pieces
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 3 tbsp red curry paste
  • 1 can (14 oz) full-fat coconut milk
  • 2 cups chicken broth
  • 6 oz egg noodles
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 cup snap peas, trimmed
  • 1 lime, juiced, plus wedges for serving
  • Fresh cilantro, for serving

Method

  1. Season the chicken with salt and pepper. Heat oil in a large pot over medium-high heat and sear the chicken 4-5 minutes, until browned. Remove and set aside.

  2. Lower the heat and add garlic, ginger, and curry paste to the pot, stirring for a minute until fragrant.

  3. Pour in the coconut milk and chicken broth, scraping up anything stuck to the bottom. Bring to a simmer and return the chicken to the pot, cooking 10 minutes until cooked through.

  4. Add the egg noodles and simmer 4-5 minutes until just tender. Stir in the cherry tomatoes and snap peas and cook 2 minutes more, until the tomatoes soften and the peas are bright green.

  5. Stir in the lime juice, then ladle into bowls and top with cilantro and lime wedges.

Snap peas go in last so they keep their crunch — don't let the soup simmer long after adding them.

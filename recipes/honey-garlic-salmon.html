Honey Garlic Salmon

Four ingredients you already have, a hot pan, and a glaze that reduces to sticky in under five minutes.

🐟
Save to Pinterest
5mPrep
15mCook
20mTotal
2Servings

Ingredients

  • 2 salmon fillets, skin on
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 scallion, sliced, for serving

Method

  1. Pat the salmon dry and season with salt. Heat olive oil in a pan over medium-high heat.

  2. Sear the salmon skin-side down for 4 minutes, flip, and cook 3 more minutes until just cooked through. Remove from the pan.

  3. Whisk honey, soy sauce, garlic, and rice vinegar together. Pour into the same pan and simmer 2-3 minutes until it thickens into a glaze.

  4. Return the salmon to the pan, spooning the glaze over the top for a minute to coat.

  5. Top with sliced scallion and serve over rice.

Swap the salmon for chicken thighs using the same glaze and timing — it works just as well.

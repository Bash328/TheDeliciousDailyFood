Mixed Berry Smoothie Bowl

Frozen fruit and a splash of milk, blended thick enough to eat with a spoon instead of drink through a straw.

Save to Pinterest
10mPrep
10mTotal
2Servings

Ingredients

  • 2 cups frozen mixed berries (strawberries and blueberries)
  • 1 ripe banana, plus more sliced for topping
  • 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 1/4 cup milk, plus more as needed
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1/2 cup granola
  • 1/4 cup fresh blueberries
  • 1/4 cup fresh strawberries, halved
  • 1 tsp chia seeds

Method

  1. Add the frozen berries, banana, yogurt, milk, and honey to a blender. Blend until thick and smooth, adding a splash more milk only if it won't move.

  2. Divide the smoothie between two bowls.

  3. Arrange sliced banana, blueberries, and strawberries over the top.

  4. Finish with granola and a scatter of chia seeds. Serve immediately with a spoon.

Keep the liquid to a minimum — the whole point is a bowl thick enough to hold its toppings.

*Last updated: August 31, 2026*

This Privacy Policy explains what information The Delicious Daily
("this site", "we") collects from visitors, how it's used, and the choices
available to you. This is a general-purpose policy and not a substitute for
legal advice — review it against your specific setup and local regulations
before relying on it.

## Information We Collect

We don't require an account or collect personal information to read
recipes. Information may still be collected automatically or through
third-party services embedded on the site, including:

- **Usage data** (pages visited, approximate location, device/browser type)
  via Google Analytics, if enabled.
- **Advertising cookies** set by Google AdSense and its partners, if ads are
  enabled, used to serve and measure ads.
- **Pinterest widgets** (the "Save" button) may set cookies from
  pinterest.com when used.
- **Grocery list data** you build on the [Grocery List
  tool](/tools/grocery-list/) is stored only in your own browser's
  local storage — it is never sent to us or to any server.

## Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. Third-party services
embedded on this site (Google Analytics, Google AdSense, Pinterest) may set
their own cookies, governed by their own privacy policies:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Pinterest Privacy Policy](https://policy.pinterest.com/privacy-policy)

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any
time.

## Advertising

This site may participate in the Google AdSense program. Google, as a
third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this
and other websites. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting
[Google's Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).

## Affiliate Links

Some links on this site are affiliate links, including links to Amazon
through the Amazon Associates program. See the [Contact & Disclosure
page](/contact/) for details. Clicking an affiliate link may set a
tracking cookie used to attribute a purchase; it does not affect the price
you pay.

## Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on
this page with a revised "last updated" date.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the [Contact

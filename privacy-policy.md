*Last updated: November 22, 2026*

This Privacy Policy explains what information The Delicious Daily
("this site", "we") collects from visitors, how it's used, and the choices
available to you. This is a general-purpose policy and not a substitute for
legal advice — review it against your specific setup and local regulations
before relying on it.

## Information We Collect

We don't require an account or collect personal information to read
recipes. Information may still be collected automatically, through
third-party services embedded on the site, or if you send us a recipe,
including:

- **Usage data** (pages visited, approximate location, device/browser type)
  via Google Analytics and Cloudflare Web Analytics.
- **Recipe submissions.** If you send in a recipe through the [Submit a
  Recipe](/submit-recipe/) form, we receive the recipe text and photo you
  send, plus the optional name and Instagram handle you choose to give. The
  name and handle are shown on the recipe as your credit if it's published.
  The submission is stored as a pull request in this site's public code
  repository on GitHub, so it can be seen by anyone, and it's processed by
  Cloudflare, which runs the form's server. We don't ask for your email
  address. To have a submission removed, use the [Contact page](/contact/).
- **Reader accounts.** If you create an account, we store your email address,
  the recipes you save, and your synced grocery list, so you can use them on
  more than one device. You sign in with a one-time link emailed to you
  (sent through Resend); there is no password. A cookie keeps you signed in for
  30 days. The data is held in Cloudflare's database. You can delete your
  account, and everything stored in it, at any time from the
  [Account page](/account/). We don't use your email for anything except signing
  you in.
- **Pinterest widgets** (the "Save" button) may set cookies from
  pinterest.com when used.
- **Grocery list data** you build on the [Grocery List
  tool](/tools/grocery-list/) is stored only in your own browser's
  local storage — it is never sent to us or to any server.

This site does not currently run advertising or participate in any
affiliate programs. If that changes, this policy will be updated to
reflect it.

## Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. Third-party services
embedded on this site (Google Analytics, Pinterest) may set their own
cookies, governed by their own privacy policies:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/)
- [Pinterest Privacy Policy](https://policy.pinterest.com/privacy-policy)

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any
time.

## Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on
this page with a revised "last updated" date.
